What data governance defines:
- <span style="color:rgb(216, 203, 251)">Who owns</span> the data.
- <span style="color:rgb(216, 203, 251)">Who can access</span> the data.
- What <span style="color:rgb(216, 203, 251)">quality standards </span>the data must meet.
# Core pieces of governance
- <span style="color:rgb(216, 203, 251)">Every dataset should have a designated owner</span>, typically a business stakeholder who understands what the data represents and is <span style="color:rgb(216, 203, 251)">accountable for its quality</span>.
- There should be <span style="color:rgb(216, 203, 251)">clearly defined data quality standards</span>, including:
	- How fresh the data needs to be.
	- Who should be alerted if something breaks.
	- The acceptable null rate for each column.
